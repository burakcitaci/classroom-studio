Ecosystems: In a food chain, arrows show the direction of energy transfer. Green plants such as clover are producers. Rabbits eat plants and foxes can eat rabbits.

Drought can reduce grass growth. A shortage of plant food can reduce rabbit populations. Foxes may have less prey as a result.

Decomposers such as fungi break down dead leaves, wood, and organisms. Nutrients returned to the soil can be reused by plants.

Pollination: Insects can carry pollen from one flower to another. Insect visits can help some plants produce seeds and fruit.

In a pond, aquatic plants are food and shelter for some animals. Light is needed for photosynthesis. An extended change in light can affect the pond food web.

Energy flows through an ecosystem. Matter, including nutrients, cycles between living things and the environment.
